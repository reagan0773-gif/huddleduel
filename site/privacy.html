Privacy Policy

Effective November 1, 2026

Huddle Duel is a game made by Reagan Britt. This policy explains what happens to information when you play it. The short version: Huddle Duel does not collect, store, or share any personal data.

Information we collect

None. The game has no accounts, sign-in, advertising, analytics, or tracking. It does not ask for your name, email address, location, contacts, photos, or any other personal information.

Information stored on your device

To save your progress, the game keeps your current season, any game in progress, your win–loss record, team name, and sound setting on your phone. This information never leaves your device, and we cannot see it. Deleting the app removes it.

Network use

Huddle Duel works offline and does not send information over the internet.

Third parties

The game contains no third-party advertising or analytics services. Apple and Google may collect information under their own privacy policies when you download apps from their stores.

Children

Because the game collects no personal information, it collects none from children.

Changes to this policy

If the game ever changes how it handles information, this page will be updated and the effective date above will change before the new version is released.
