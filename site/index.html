Common questions

How do I play?
On offense, tap a play. The defense picks a formation in secret, then both are revealed and two dice decide the result. Tap Rules in the game for a guide to which plays beat which defenses.
Is my season saved?
Yes. Your season, game in progress, and record are saved on your phone automatically. Deleting the app also deletes this saved progress.
Does it need an internet connection?
No. Huddle Duel works completely offline.
I don't hear any sound.
Check that your phone isn't on silent and that the speaker button at the top of the game is on.
How do I play against a friend?
Tap New, choose Vs. friend, and pass the phone back and forth. Each player calls a play while the other looks away.
